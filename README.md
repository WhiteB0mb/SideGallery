# SideGallery 🖼️

> **Your favorite images and GIFs at your fingertips, over any app!**

---

## 💡 What is SideGallery & Why do you need it?

Ever wanted to quickly send a meme, reaction GIF, sticker, or screenshot while chatting on WhatsApp, Discord, or Telegram, without having to leave your current app and dig through your gallery?

**SideGallery** brings a **collapsible sidebar overlay** (or a **floating bubble**) that you can open on top of any application with a single tap or swipe. Copy and paste your media in seconds without interrupting whatever you are doing!

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📂 **Custom Folder Support**: Pick any folder on your device containing your favorite pictures, screenshots, or GIFs.
- 📋 **1-Tap Quick Copy**: Tap any image or GIF to instantly copy it to your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.
- 👆 **Flexible Trigger Options**:
  - **Floating Bubble**: A draggable floating button you can place anywhere along the screen edge.
  - **Edge Swipe**: Completely invisible trigger; simply swipe inward from the screen edge.
- 👻 **Smart Inactivity Fade (Idle Fade)**: When left untouched for 3 seconds, the floating bubble smoothly fades to 35% opacity so it won’t block your reading or videos.
- 🔄 **Auto-Hide in Landscape**: Automatically hides the overlay when watching videos or playing games in landscape mode.
- 🗑️ **Quick Delete Gesture**: Long-press any item to reveal a fast delete confirmation (automatically dismisses after 3 seconds if untouched).
- ➕ **In-Overlay Media Import**: Add new images or GIFs directly through the `+` button in the sidebar.
- 🎨 **Deep Customization**:
  - **Light, Dark, or System Theme** toggle.
  - Choose between **Left or Right screen dock**.
  - Adjust panel width (**1/3, 1/2, or 2/3 of the screen**).
  - Configure grid columns (1 to 4 columns).
  - Sort media by name, date modified, or file size.
- 🔒 **100% Offline & Private**: Zero internet permissions. All your files stay strictly on your device.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

1. **Select Folder**: Open SideGallery and tap **"Select Image Folder"** to grant access to your chosen media folder.
2. **Grant Permissions**:
   - **Display Over Other Apps (Overlay)**: Required to show the sidebar over other apps.
   - **Disable Battery Restrictions**: Ensures Android keeps the service alive reliably in the background.
3. **Start Service**: Turn on **"Enable Sidebar Overlay"** and enjoy instant access to your gallery anywhere!
